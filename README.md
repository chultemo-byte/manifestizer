# Manifestizer

Public name: Manifestizer (`manifestizer.com`)
Circuit inside: Praxis

Science mechanics from idea to fulfillment.

A practice engine — not a law of attraction app.

## Five laws

1. State before scene.
2. Filmable or it is not locked.
3. Process, not trophy.
4. Obstacle before act.
5. Evidence before the next wish.

Breath → thought → emotion → vision → imagination → feeling → contrast → act → fulfillment.

Fantasy without contrast is blocked. Action is required. Fulfillment is evidence.

## Honesty

This does not summon outcomes. It regulates the nervous system, tags attention, simulates a process, contrasts the wish against an inner obstacle, commits one tiny move, and reviews what actually happened.

The field does not clock in. God will not lift a finger. You do.

## Circuit

1. **Idea** — a scene a camera could film
2. **Breath** — resonance pacing ~6 breaths/min
3. **Thought** — a controllable goal sentence
4. **Emotion** — the feeling you will carry into the work
5. **Vision** — first-person process imagery
6. **Imagination** — one episodic future scene
7. **Feeling** — where the future sits in the body
8. **Contrast** — WOOP (wish, outcome, obstacle, if-then)
9. **Act** — after [anchor], I will [tiny behavior]
10. **Fulfillment** — yes / partial / no, then adjust

Data lives in `localStorage` under `praxis:v1`. Export and import from the log.
